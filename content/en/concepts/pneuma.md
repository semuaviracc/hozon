> *"The wind (πνεῦμα) blows where it wishes... So it is with everyone who is born of the Spirit." — John 3:8*

**Pneuma** (πνεῦμα) is the very Breath of God at work — not an energy or force that can be trained. Many ancient traditions used the word "breath" for the principle that gives life: Stoic philosophy with its *pneuma*, the Yogic tradition with *prana*, Chinese cosmology with *qi*. All of them stop at the same point — for them it remains a something that can be studied and harnessed through technique.

Pneuma is fundamentally different: He is a Person, not a substance or energy. From before the first word was spoken, the Spirit of God was already hovering over the waters — present long before there was anything to enliven. If the Living One is the source and His love is the content, then Pneuma is the movement that makes that love active.

Through Pneuma, the divine life is shared and comes to dwell within those who receive Him — transferring humanity from the power of death to the power of the Living One, bringing holiness and life at once.
