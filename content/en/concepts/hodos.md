> *"I am the way (ὁδός)..." — John 14:6*

**Hodos** is the direction and the journey toward the fullness of life.

God not only reveals the destination but becomes the way to it. Hodos shows that life with God is not a static state, but a journey that is always underway.

Through Hodos, humanity is led toward likeness to the Living One.
