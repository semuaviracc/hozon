> *"In the beginning was the Word (λόγος), and the Word was with God..." — John 1:1*

**Logos** (λόγος) is the full self-expression of the Living One — not merely a spoken word, but God Himself making Himself known. Six centuries before Christ, Greek philosophers already used this word for the invisible rational principle that governs the universe; yet for them the logos remained a force, not a person who could be addressed.

The Gospel of John borrowed that familiar word, then detonated it with a claim no one had anticipated: *"the Word became flesh."* The Logos was no longer a principle ordering things from afar, but the mind and love of God that could be seen, heard, and touched.

Just as the creating word — *"let there be light"* — brought all things into being, so from the Living One flows the Logos, and from the Logos radiates light. The Word never points to itself; it always unveils the One who speaks.
