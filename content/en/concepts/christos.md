> *"He is the image of the invisible God, the firstborn of all creation..." — Colossians 1:15*

**Christos** (Χριστός) is not merely one word standing alongside Word, Light, or Life. He is the destination where all that revelation — love, light, life, word, truth, way, glory — converges and is unveiled in full within human history.

Some set Christ alongside ancient gods who died and rose with the turning of the seasons. But those myths turn in cycles, with no claim to a historical event; Christ, by contrast, is one unrepeatable historical event, sealed by His final word on the cross — *"it is finished"* — once and for all.

*"In him the whole fullness of deity dwells bodily,"* wrote Paul; *"Whoever has seen me has seen the Father,"* said Jesus Himself. Christ does not speak from outside the Living One like a messenger carrying word from afar — He manifests the life of the Living One itself, without remainder, without distance.
