> *"I am the Living One (ὁ ζῶν). I died, and behold I am alive forevermore, and I hold the keys of Death and Hades." — Revelation 1:18*

**Ho Zon** (ὁ ζῶν — the Living One) is the center of this entire revelation, a name that precedes every definition. Before the word "God" was ever burdened with philosophy, before layered theological formulas existed, there was a Person who was present, who spoke, who guided, who promised.

Ho Zon is not a philosophical concept of "That Which Is" — a pure, static, cold existence. He transcends every category of "being," not because He is less real, but because He is too full to be confined within any human-made definition. He is the Source from which every unveiling — love, light, life, word — flows.

The book of Revelation unveils this most clearly. Christ says: *"I am the First and the Last... and the Living One. I died, and behold I am alive forevermore."* The One who Is, it turns out, is not merely Being — He Lives, and He proved it not with an argument but by dying and rising. In the end, Ho Zon is not the answer to the question "what is existence," but to a far more fundamental one: who lives, and who shares that life with us.
