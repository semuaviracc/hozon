> *"I am the way, and the truth (ἀλήθεια), and the life..." — John 14:6*

**Aletheia** (ἀλήθεια) is usually translated "truth," but its original meaning is sharper: "the unhidden." Truth, first of all, is not a matter of a statement matching the facts, but of God opening Himself fully with nothing concealed.

When Jesus called Himself *"the truth"* (John 14:6), He was showing God without a veil, without any hidden remainder. That is why the opposite of truth is not merely a mistaken statement, but a lie — a word turned inside out into its own negation.

Aletheia is the "seal" side of unveiling: the guarantee that what God shows about Himself really is Himself, exact and without distortion. It is paired with glory (*Doxa*) — the one ensures the unveiling is exact, the other ensures it radiates and is seen.
