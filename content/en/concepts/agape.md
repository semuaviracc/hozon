> *"So we have come to know and to believe the love that God has for us. God is love, and whoever abides in love abides in God, and God abides in him." — 1 John 4:16*

**Agape** (ἀγάπη) is love that flows downward from Life itself — not a love that ascends to pursue something because it is worthy, but a love that gives precisely when the beloved is not worthy at all. *"In this is love, not that we loved God but that he loved us."*

Agape is not an emotion arising in response, nor merely a soft, unconditional affection. It is a movement far more fundamental: the outflow of the Living One Himself. If the Living One is the spring, Agape is the stream that is inseparable from its source.

True love always ends in life given. Its summit is the cross — not merely a symbol of suffering, but the symbol of Agape itself: the life of God giving Himself away to bring life to others.
