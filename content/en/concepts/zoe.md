> *"In him was life (ζωή), and the life was the light of men." — John 1:4*

**Zoe** (ζωή) is the very life of God, shared — not merely a long biological existence. In everyday usage, "life" is the breath that plants, animals, and humans all possess as a matter of course. Zoe is a different kind: not the life anyone has by default, but the quality of life that flows directly from the Living One.

When Jesus said, *"I came that they may have life,"* He was not offering an extension of breath, but sharing that divine life itself. Love (*Agape*) is its giver; Zoe is the life given.

Strangely, this life can only be received through one unexpected door: death. As long as the old life still reigns, there is no room for Zoe. Only through the death and resurrection of Christ is this realm of life opened — the life of God given to those who dare to pass through that narrow door with Him.
